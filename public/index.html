Digital safety guide

Protect Yourself From Online Scams

A concise guide to recognizing risks, reducing your digital footprint, and keeping control of personal information.

How phishing works

Scammers imitate trusted brands or people to create urgency and get you to reveal a password, code, or payment detail.

Information collection

Forms, giveaways, breached accounts, and public posts can be combined into a detailed profile of a person.

Suspicious websites

Check the full domain, contact details, spelling, payment methods, and whether the offer feels unusually urgent or generous.

Fraudulent links

Hover or press and hold to inspect a link. Look for misspellings, extra words, strange subdomains, and shortened URLs.

Personal data risks

Dates of birth, addresses, documents, and security answers can enable account takeover and identity fraud when exposed.

IP addresses and footprints

Web servers receive an IP address to respond to a visit. Browser settings and permission choices affect what additional data a site can access.

Protect your identity

Use a password manager, unique passwords, multi-factor authentication, updates, careful sharing, and independent verification of requests.
